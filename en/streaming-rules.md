## 1. Official channel

The **only official broadcast channel** for the tournament is:

**[twitch.tv/traversalgame_fr](https://twitch.tv/traversalgame_fr)**

The broadcast is run by the **production team** appointed by the organisers, under the authority of the admins.

---

## 2. Consent to broadcasting

### 2.1 Acceptance through registration

By registering for the tournament, each player **agrees that their games may be broadcast live**, together with their **username**, their **Discord profile picture** and, if they provide it, their **camera feed**, on the official channel and on Traversal's official channels (replays, clips).

### 2.2 What is and is not optional

- The player's **camera** is **optional** (§ 6).
- **Broadcasting the game itself** is **not optional**: a player may not refuse it, oppose it or obstruct it (§ 7 and § 9).

A series is **designated for broadcast** once it is listed in § 3.1 or an admin has announced it under § 3.2. Only the admins can **lift** that designation.

## 4. Timing of broadcast series

### 4.1 Reference durations

| Item | Duration |
|---|:-:|
| Expected average length of a game | **15 min** |
| Break between two games of the same series | **5 min** |
| Break between two series | **10 min** |
| Maximum planned length of a BO5 (5 games + 4 breaks) | **95 min** |
| Maximum planned length of a BO3 (3 games + 2 breaks) | **55 min** |

The end times in § 3.1 assume every series goes to a 5th game. A series may finish earlier; the following series nevertheless keeps its time H, unless an admin decides otherwise.

### 4.2 Clock

Each game is played with **10 minutes on the clock per player**, in accordance with § 7.2 of the tournament rules.

### 4.3 Lateness

The lateness and forfeit rules of the tournament rules (§ 9) apply **unchanged** to broadcast series. A delay caused by the production team cannot result in any forfeit (§ 8).

---

## 5. Player obligations

### 5.1 Being reachable

- Players in a broadcast series must be **online and reachable on the official Discord server** no later than **15 minutes before time H**, and remain so **throughout the series**.
- **No voice channel is required.**

### 5.2 Starting each game

1. The player who creates the game sends **the game link** to the production team and to their opponent on Discord.
2. The game complies with **the mandatory settings** in the tournament rules (§ 7.2): **public** visibility, **unranked**, **Random** terrain, **10 minutes per player** on the clock.
3. The players **wait for the go-ahead from the production team or an admin** before playing the first move.

### 5.3 No stream sniping

Throughout the series, players may not **watch the broadcast** or the **spectator view** of their own game, or receive any information from them (§ 13.2 of the tournament rules).

---

## 6. Player cameras

### 6.1 Optional camera

- Each player may **optionally** provide their camera feed during their broadcast games.
- **Without a camera**, the production team shows the player's **Discord profile picture** instead.
- Choosing not to provide a camera carries **no sanction**.

### 6.2 Transmission

- The camera feed is sent **only through a VDO.Ninja link provided by the admins or the production team**.
- A player who wants to provide their camera informs an admin **before the day of their series**, then connects to the link provided **no later than 15 minutes before time H** for a test.
- A camera problem justifies **neither a delay nor a pause**. The production team then switches to the Discord profile picture.

### 6.3 Minors

A minor may provide their camera feed only with the **consent of their parents or legal guardians**. The admins may ask for confirmation of this before the broadcast.

### 6.4 Content

- The camera image must remain **decent and respectful**: no offensive, discriminatory, sexual or unauthorised advertising content.
- The camera must **not show any screen** displaying the broadcast or the game.
- The production team may **cut** a player's camera at any time. Inappropriate content is sanctioned under § 14 of the tournament rules.

### 6.5 Discord profile picture

The Discord profile picture displayed must follow the same content rules (§ 6.4). Otherwise, the production team replaces it with a neutral image and the player may be sanctioned.

## 7. Broadcast integrity

For any series **designated for broadcast** (§ 3.3), it is **prohibited**:

1. to **refuse to let the game itself be broadcast**;
2. to **prevent or obstruct the broadcast** (private game, link not sent, game restarted out of the production team's view, etc.);
3. to **refuse the broadcast on the official channel in order to broadcast it elsewhere**, or to broadcast the series on another channel or platform **without the admins' permission**;
4. to **play a game off stream without the admins' approval**, in particular by starting a game before the production team's go-ahead (§ 5.2).

---

## 8. Broadcast incidents

- If the **production team has a technical problem**, the admins may **delay the start** of a game or series, or exceptionally **allow** the game to be played off stream.
- A delay decided by the admins **cannot result in any forfeit**: time H and the reference times announced by the admin replace the scheduled ones (§ 6.3 and § 9.6 of the tournament rules).
- A **broadcast outage during a game** does not interrupt the game, unless an admin instructs otherwise. The result remains valid.
- Issues with the game itself (disconnection, site bug) are governed by § 11 of the tournament rules.

---

## 9. Sanctions

### 9.1 Disqualification

Any breach of **§ 7** (refusing or obstructing the broadcast of the game, broadcasting anywhere other than the official channel, playing a game off stream without the admins' approval) results in the **disqualification of the player or players involved**, under § 15.3 of the tournament rules.

### 9.2 Other breaches

Other breaches of these rules (being reachable, camera content, stream sniping, etc.) are sanctioned according to the **graduated scale** in § 15 of the tournament rules. Stream sniping is **cheating** and results in immediate disqualification.

### 9.3 What is never sanctioned

**Not providing a camera** is **never** sanctioned.
