## 1. General provisions

### 1.1 Purpose

These rules govern the **Traversal Rivals 2026** tournament (the "tournament"), played on **Traversal**, online, on the official website [traversalgame.com](https://traversalgame.com).

### 1.2 Acceptance

Registering for the tournament constitutes **full and unconditional acceptance** of these rules, of the [game rules](traversal-rules.md) and of the [broadcast rules](streaming-rules.md). No player may claim ignorance of these documents.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Game** | **One match** of Traversal, from the first move to the end of the game. |
| **Series** (or **BO**, *Best Of*) | A set of games between **two players**. A series consists of **one or more games** and ends **as soon as one player has reached the required number of wins**. |
| **BO3** (*Best of 3*) | A series of **up to 3 games**: the **first player to 2 wins** takes the series. Possible final scores: 2-0 or 2-1. |
| **BO5** (*Best of 5*) | A series of **up to 5 games**: the **first player to 3 wins** takes the series. Possible final scores: 3-0, 3-1 or 3-2. |
| **Deciding game** | An extra game played to replace a game still tied after § 10.3 has been applied. It does not change the number of wins required. |
| **Upper Bracket** | The main bracket. Every player starts here. A loss here sends the player down to the Lower Bracket. |
| **Lower Bracket** | The second-chance bracket. A loss here means **elimination**. |
| **Grand Final** | The final series between the Upper Bracket winner and the Lower Bracket winner. |
| **Bye** | An empty slot in the bracket. The player facing a bye advances to the next round without playing. |
| **Forfeit** | Losing a game, or a series, without it being played (§ 9). |
| **Time H** | The official start time of a series (§ 6, or the time announced by an admin). |
| **Admin** | A member of the tournament administration team (§ 17). |

> **Example:** a BO5 series won 3-1 consists of **4 games**. The 5th game is not played.

## 3. Registration and eligibility

### 3.1 Registration period

- **Opens:** Monday 23 November 2026.
- **Closes:** Saturday 28 November 2026 at **23:59**.
- **Places:** **32 players maximum**, allocated **in order of registration** (first come, first served). Any registration beyond the 32nd place is refused.

### 3.2 Procedure

Registration takes place **only on the official Discord server, through the tournament's Discord bot**. It is valid only if the player has **linked their traversalgame.com account** to their Discord account through that bot.

### 3.3 Eligibility requirements

To take part, each player must:

1. have a personal **traversalgame.com account**;
2. have a personal **Discord account** and be a **member of the official Discord server** throughout the tournament;
3. have **linked** these two accounts through the Discord bot (§ 3.2).

### 3.4 One player, one account

- Each person may register **only once** and play with **only one traversalgame.com account**: the one linked at registration.
- **Multi-accounting** (several registrations by the same person) is **prohibited**.
- **Account sharing** (an account played by anyone other than its owner, even occasionally) is **prohibited**.
- A player cannot be replaced during the tournament.

### 3.5 Minors

- Minors may take part **without prior parental consent**.
- **Exception:** a minor may turn on their **camera** during a broadcast series only with the **consent of their parents or legal guardians** (see the [broadcast rules](streaming-rules.md)).

### 3.6 Withdrawal

A player who wishes to withdraw must inform the admins on the Discord server **as early as possible**. A vacated place **is not reallocated**: it becomes a bye (§ 4.3).

## 4. Check-in and bracket draw

### 4.1 Bracket draw

- The bracket is drawn **entirely at random**: there are no seeds and no ranking is taken into account.
- The bracket is **published on Challonge on Sunday 29 November 2026**. No games are played that day.

### 4.2 Check-in

- Every registered player must confirm their attendance (**check-in**) **through the Discord bot**.
- Check-in opens when the bracket is published and **closes on Monday 30 November 2026 at 19:00**, when the first series begin.

### 4.3 Empty places (byes)

- If there are **fewer than 32 registered players** when registration closes, or if a player withdraws **before the draw**, the vacant places become **byes**, placed by the random draw.
- A player facing a bye in **Upper Round 1** advances directly to **Upper Round 2**. The corresponding place in **Lower Round 1** then also becomes a bye.
- A player facing a bye in the **Lower Bracket** advances directly to the next round.
- A bye counts as neither a win nor a loss for anyone.

### 4.4 Missed check-in or withdrawal after the draw

A player who has not checked in by **Monday 30 November at 19:00**, or who withdraws after the bracket is published and before their first series:

- is **removed from the tournament** (they do **not** drop to the Lower Bracket);
- their Upper Round 1 opponent **wins the series 2-0 by forfeit**;
- their place in Lower Round 1 becomes a **bye**.

## 5. Tournament format

### 5.1 Overview

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 32 maximum |
| Format | **Double elimination** |
| BO3 series | All series except those listed below |
| BO5 series | **Upper Semifinals**, **Upper Final**, **Lower Semifinal**, **Lower Final**, **Grand Final** |
| Grand Final reset | **No** (no bracket reset) |

### 5.2 How double elimination works

- All players start in the **Upper Bracket**.
- A player who **loses a series in the Upper Bracket** drops to the **Lower Bracket**.
- A player who **loses a series in the Lower Bracket** is **eliminated** from the tournament.
- Each player can therefore lose **one series** before the Grand Final without being eliminated.
- A series lost **by forfeit** (§ 9) is a **normal loss** and follows the same rules.

### 5.3 Bracket structure

**Upper Bracket**

| Round | Series | Format | Winners | Losers |
|---|:-:|:-:|---|---|
| Upper Round 1 | 16 | BO3 | → Upper Round 2 | → Lower Round 1 |
| Upper Round 2 | 8 | BO3 | → Upper Quarterfinals | → Lower Round 2 |
| Upper Quarterfinals | 4 | BO3 | → Upper Semifinals | → Lower Round 4 |
| Upper Semifinals | 2 | **BO5** | → Upper Final | → Lower Round 6 |
| Upper Final | 1 | **BO5** | → **Grand Final** | → Lower Final |

**Lower Bracket**

| Round | Series | Format | Opponents | Winners | Losers |
|---|:-:|:-:|---|---|---|
| Lower Round 1 | 8 | BO3 | the 16 losers of Upper Round 1 | → Lower Round 2 | eliminated |
| Lower Round 2 | 8 | BO3 | Lower Round 1 winners **vs** Upper Round 2 losers | → Lower Round 3 | eliminated |
| Lower Round 3 | 4 | BO3 | Lower Round 2 winners | → Lower Round 4 | eliminated |
| Lower Round 4 | 4 | BO3 | Lower Round 3 winners **vs** Upper Quarterfinal losers | → Lower Round 5 | eliminated |
| Lower Round 5 | 2 | BO3 | Lower Round 4 winners | → Lower Round 6 | eliminated |
| Lower Round 6 | 2 | BO3 | Lower Round 5 winners **vs** Upper Semifinal losers | → Lower Semifinal | eliminated |
| Lower Semifinal | 1 | **BO5** | Lower Round 6 winners | → Lower Final | eliminated |
| Lower Final | 1 | **BO5** | Lower Semifinal winner **vs** Upper Final loser | → **Grand Final** | eliminated |

**Grand Final**

| Series | Format | Opponents |
|---|:-:|---|
| Grand Final | **BO5** | Upper Final winner **vs** Lower Final winner |

The exact placement of players dropping from the Upper Bracket into the Lower Bracket is the one shown on **the Challonge bracket, which is authoritative**.

### 5.4 Grand Final without reset

- The Grand Final is played as **a single BO5 series**.
- **Its winner is crowned champion**, whether they come from the Upper Bracket or the Lower Bracket.
- The player coming from the Upper Bracket **has no advantage**: there is **no second series** (*bracket reset*) if the player from the Lower Bracket wins.

### 5.5 Final standings

| Place | Player(s) |
|:-:|---|
| 1st | Grand Final winner |
| 2nd | Grand Final loser |
| 3rd | Lower Final loser |
| 4th | Lower Semifinal loser |
| 5th – 6th | Lower Round 6 losers |
| 7th – 8th | Lower Round 5 losers |
| 9th – 12th | Lower Round 4 losers |
| 13th – 16th | Lower Round 3 losers |
| 17th – 24th | Lower Round 2 losers |
| 25th – 32nd | Lower Round 1 losers |

Players within the same group share that placing **equally**.

## 7. Mandatory game settings

### 7.1 Venue

**Every game** is played **exclusively on the official website [traversalgame.com](https://traversalgame.com)**, using the account linked at registration (§ 3.2). No game played anywhere else (another website, a modified client, a physical board, etc.) is recognised.

### 7.2 Game settings

Every game must be created with **all** of the following settings:

| Setting | Required value |
|---|---|
| Visibility | **Public** (admins and the production team must be able to view the game) |
| Game type | **Unranked** |
| Terrain mode | **Random** (board generated by the site) |
| Clock | **10 minutes per player** for the whole game |
| Side assignment (Player 1 red / Player 2 blue) | **Random, assigned by the site** |

### 7.3 Creating the game

- By default, the game is created by the player listed **at the top** of the series on Challonge, who sends the link to their opponent on Discord. The players may agree that the other player creates it instead.
- **Both players must check the settings** before the first move is played.

### 7.4 Game played with non-compliant settings

- If the mistake is noticed **before the first move**, the game is simply recreated with the correct settings, **with no sanction**.
- If a game has been played (even partly) with **one or more settings that do not comply** with § 7.2:
  - the game **does not count**, whatever its result;
  - it is **replayed** with the correct settings;
  - **each of the two players receives a warning** (§ 15), since checking is the responsibility of both.

## 8. How a series is played

### 8.1 Before the series

- Both players must be **online and reachable on the Discord server** by **time H** at the latest.
- The players get in touch on Discord, create the game (§ 7.3) and check its settings (§ 7.2).

### 8.2 Sequence of games

1. **Game 1** starts at **time H**.
2. After each game, the players have a **break of up to 5 minutes**; the next game starts no later than the end of that break. The players may agree to shorten the break.
3. Each game is played in a **new game lobby**, created in accordance with § 7. The site assigns sides at random again.
4. The series ends **as soon as one player reaches the required number of wins** (2 in a BO3, 3 in a BO5).

### 8.3 Applicable game rules

Each game is governed by the [game rules](traversal-rules.md), subject to the provisions of these rules (settings in § 7, tiebreakers in § 10). If the written game rules differ from how the site works, **the site's behaviour prevails**.

### 8.4 Resigning

A player may **resign a game** at any time, using the site's resign function. Resigning counts as a **loss** for that game only. Deliberately resigning games to fix a result is prohibited (§ 13).

## 9. Punctuality, lateness and forfeits

### 9.1 Reference start time of each game

Each game in a series has a **reference start time**:

| Game | Reference start time |
|---|---|
| Game 1 | **Time H** |
| Game following a game that was **played** | **End of the previous game + 5 minutes** (break) |
| Game following a game **lost by forfeit** | **Reference time of the forfeited game + 20 minutes** (15 min game length + 5 min break) |

### 9.2 Lateness allowance: 5 minutes

- A player has a **5-minute allowance** after the reference start time of each game.
- A player is considered **present** when they are **reachable on Discord and ready to play** (in the game, or able to join it immediately).
- A player who is not present **5 minutes after the reference start time** of a game **loses that game by forfeit**. Their opponent must report it immediately to an admin, who records the forfeit.
- A late player who arrives **before** the allowance runs out plays the game as normal.

### 9.3 Successive forfeits

Forfeits apply **game by game**. Example for a series with time H at 19:00 where player B does not show up:

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| H+0 (19:00) | Reference time of game 1 |
| **H+5** (19:05) | Player B absent → **game 1 lost by forfeit** (1-0) |
| H+20 (19:20) | Reference time of game 2 |
| **H+25** (19:25) | Player B absent → **game 2 lost by forfeit** (2-0) → **BO3 over** |
| H+40 (19:40) | *(BO5 only)* Reference time of game 3 |
| **H+45** (19:45) | *(BO5 only)* Player B absent → **game 3 lost by forfeit** (3-0) → **BO5 over** |

If player B turns up at, say, 19:12, they have lost game 1 but **play game 2**, which starts at 19:20 at the latest. The two players may agree to start it earlier.

### 9.4 Consequences of a forfeit

- A game lost by forfeit counts as a **win** for the opponent.
- A series lost by forfeit (in whole or in part) is a **normal loss** (§ 5.2): dropping to the Lower Bracket from the Upper Bracket, elimination from the Lower Bracket.
- Repeated or deliberate forfeits may also be sanctioned under § 15.

### 9.5 Both players absent

If **neither player** is present when an allowance runs out, an admin rules on the outcome of the series (forfeit by one player, by both, or a new time H).

### 9.6 Delays caused by the organisers

No forfeit may be declared when the delay is caused by the organisers (previous slot not finished, production issue, admin decision). Time H as announced by the admin then applies (§ 6.3).

## 10. Game results and tiebreakers

### 10.1 Result of a game

The result of a game is the one **determined by the site** at the end of the game, in accordance with the [game rules](traversal-rules.md): Sentinel special victory, standard victory on the 2nd piece exited, loss for lack of a legal move, **loss on time** (clock runs out), resignation, or the 200-move limit.

### 10.2 Principle: there is always a winner

**No game can end in a draw in the tournament.** When the site declares a game **drawn** (tied score on the 2nd piece exited, or a complete tie at the 200-move limit), the winner of the game is decided by the **tiebreak procedure** in § 10.3, applied to the **final position** of the game.

### 10.3 Tiebreak procedure

The criteria are applied **in order**. As soon as one criterion separates the players, the procedure stops.

| Order | Criterion | The game is won by the player who has… |
|:-:|---|---|
| **1** | Remaining Strength | the **highest total Base Strength** of their pieces **still on the board** |
| **2** | Number of pieces | the **most pieces still on the board** |
| **3** | Distance | the most advanced piece **closest to its exit**, counted in rows still to cross (ignoring terrain) |
| **4** | Strength of the most advanced piece | if distances are equal, the most advanced piece with the **highest Base Strength** |
| **5** | Time remaining | the **most time left on their clock** at the end of the game |
| **6** | Deciding game | — if no criterion separates the players, the drawn game **does not count** and a **deciding game** is played |

**Clarifications:**

- Pieces that have **exited** the board are not taken into account in criteria 1 to 4: they have already been counted in the score shown by the site.
- **Rows still to cross** (criterion 3): for Player 1, 10 − the piece's row number; for Player 2, the piece's row number − 1. A piece on its last row is therefore at 0.
- **Criterion 4**: if several of a player's pieces are tied for the shortest distance, the one with the highest Base Strength is used.
- A criterion that cannot apply **to both players** (for example, neither player has any piece left on the board for criteria 3 and 4) is **skipped** and the next criterion is used.
- The **deciding game** is played with the same settings (§ 7) and the same rules as any other game, after the normal 5-minute break. It is itself subject to § 10. The series continues in this way until one player reaches the required number of wins.
- Where organisational constraints require it, the admins may, exceptionally, **step in to declare the winner** of an undecided game or series, or set any other tiebreak arrangement.

### 10.4 Example

The site declares a 2-2 draw (one Vanguard exited on each side). Left on the board:

| | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Remaining pieces | Sentinel, Explorer, Explorer | Sentinel, Vanguard |
| **Criterion 1 — Remaining Strength** | 3 + 1 + 1 = **5** | 3 + 2 = **5** |
| **Criterion 2 — Number of pieces** | **3** | 2 |

Criterion 1 is tied and criterion 2 separates them: **Player 1 wins the game**.

## 11. Disconnections and technical issues

### 11.1 Player responsibility

Each player is **solely responsible** for their own hardware, internet connection and playing environment. A technical problem on a player's side justifies neither a pause nor a replay.

### 11.2 Player disconnection

- If a player disconnects, **the game continues and the disconnected player's clock keeps running**.
- The player may reconnect and resume the game as long as their clock has not run out.
- If they do not return in time, they **lose the game on time**, as provided by the game rules.
- The disconnected player must **immediately notify** their opponent and an admin on Discord.

### 11.3 Confirmed site bug

- If the **site malfunctions** (as opposed to a player's hardware or connection), the players must **report it immediately** to an admin, with any available evidence (screenshot, video, game link).
- If an admin **confirms the bug**, the game is **replayed in full**, in a new game. **No position is restored.**
- If the bug is not confirmed by an admin, the result shown by the site **stands**.
- Deliberately exploiting a bug is prohibited (§ 13).

### 11.4 Site-wide outage

If traversalgame.com is unavailable for an extended period, the admins may **suspend, postpone or reorganise** the affected series. Games already completed and validated stand.

## 12. Reporting results and disputes

### 12.1 Who reports, and when

The **winner of the series** reports the result **within 10 minutes** of the end of the series, in the **Discord channel provided for this purpose**.

### 12.2 Required content of the report

The report includes:

1. the **series score** (e.g. 2-1);
2. for **each game played**:
   - the **game link** on traversalgame.com;
   - a **screenshot of the end-of-game screen**, showing the result and, where applicable, the final position and the clocks;
3. for each game **decided by tiebreak** under § 10.3: the **criterion** that decided the winner;
4. for each **forfeit**: the time it was recorded and the name of the admin who recorded it.

### 12.3 Validation

- An admin checks the report and **enters the result on Challonge**, which is the **official result**.
- The **losing player** must **check** the report and point out any error.
- A game that cannot be viewed, or whose settings do not comply with § 7.2, **does not count** (§ 7.4).

### 12.4 Disputes

- Any dispute must be filed with the admins on Discord **no later than 15 minutes after the result is reported**, together with its evidence.
- After that deadline, the result is **final**, unless cheating (§ 13) is discovered later, which can be sanctioned at any time.
- The admins rule after hearing both players where appropriate.

## 13. Fair play, cheating and prohibited tools

### 13.1 Principle

Each player plays **alone**, using their **own abilities**, in **a sporting spirit**. Any practice that gives an unfair advantage or **harms fair play** is prohibited, whether or not it is expressly listed below.

### 13.2 Cheating — prohibited practices

The following are prohibited, among others, **throughout every series**:

1. **Receiving outside help**: advice, information or analysis from another player, a spectator, a friend or anyone else, by any means (voice, message, physical presence, etc.);
2. **Using a bot, analysis engine, artificial intelligence, calculator or any other tool** that suggests, evaluates or plays moves;
3. **Watching a broadcast or the spectator view of your own game** (*stream sniping*);
4. **Letting someone else play on your account**, or playing on someone else's account (§ 3.4);
5. **Fixing a result** with another player, deliberately losing a game or a series, or colluding to influence the bracket;
6. **Deliberately exploiting a bug** in the site or the game;
7. **Deliberately disrupting** the opponent's game or connection.

### 13.3 Unofficial tools and modifications

Using any **unofficial theme, design, script, browser extension, *userscript*, modified client or software** that **affects the game in any way** is **prohibited**: display, interface, information shown, calculations, automation or game behaviour.

Only the **traversalgame.com website as officially provided** may be used. If in doubt about a tool, the player must **ask an admin for permission before their series**. Without permission, the tool is deemed prohibited.

### 13.4 Checks

The admins may examine played games and ask a player for explanations at any time. A player who **refuses to cooperate** with a check may be sanctioned as if they had committed the offence.

### 13.5 Reporting

Any player who suspects an offence must report it **privately** to an admin, with their evidence. Public accusations are not allowed (§ 14).

## 14. Conduct

Each player undertakes to:

- **respect** their opponents, the admins, the production team, the casters and the audience;
- refrain from **any insult, provocation, discriminatory remark, harassment or threat**, in game, on Discord, in the Twitch chat or anywhere else;
- comply with the **rules of the official Discord server**;
- follow the **admins' instructions**.

## 15. Sanctions

### 15.1 Graduated scale

Breaches of these rules are sanctioned according to the following scale. Sanctions **accumulate across the whole tournament**.

| Level | Sanction |
|:-:|---|
| 1 | **Warning** |
| 2 | **Loss of a game** |
| 3 | **Loss of the series** |
| 4 | **Disqualification** from the tournament |

- Each new offence moves the player up one level: the 1st offence results in a warning, the 2nd in the loss of a game, and so on.
- **Loss of a game** applies to the current game or, failing that, to the player's next game.
- **Loss of the series** applies to the current series or, failing that, to the player's next series.
- Depending on the **seriousness** of the offence, the admins may **apply a higher level directly**.

### 15.2 Immediate disqualification

The following lead to **immediate disqualification**:

- any **proven cheating** (§ 13.2);
- use of an **unofficial tool or modification** affecting the game (§ 13.3);
- **multi-accounting** or **account sharing** (§ 3.4);
- the **breaches of the broadcast rules** that those rules sanction in this way (refusing or obstructing the broadcast, broadcasting anywhere other than the official channel, playing a game off stream without approval);
- **serious breaches of § 14** (harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks).

### 15.3 Effects of disqualification

- The disqualified player is **removed from the tournament** and loses any placing.
- The current series is **lost**; their remaining series are **lost by forfeit**.
- Results of series **already completed** stand, unless the admins decide otherwise (in particular in the case of cheating).

### 15.4 Additional sanctions

For serious offences, the admins may also **ban the player from future editions** of Traversal Rivals.

## 16. Broadcasting

The conditions for broadcasting series (official channel, cameras, player obligations and related sanctions) are set out in the [**broadcast rules**](streaming-rules.md), which form an integral part of these rules.

By registering, each player **agrees that their games may be broadcast live**.

## 17. Tournament administration

### 17.1 Role of the admins

The admins are appointed by the organisers and identified by a **dedicated role on the Discord server**. They are responsible for enforcing these rules, recording forfeits and incidents, validating results, ruling on disputes and imposing sanctions.

### 17.2 Decisions

- Admin decisions are **final and not open to appeal**.
- An admin **does not rule** on a series in which they are personally involved.

### 17.3 Situations not covered

Any situation not covered by these rules is decided by the admins, **in the spirit of these rules and of fair play**.

### 17.4 Changes to the rules

The organisers may amend these rules, in particular to correct an error or deal with an unforeseen situation. Any change is **announced on the official Discord server** and takes effect on publication. It does not apply retroactively to games already completed.

## 18. Quick reference

```
FORMAT      32 players max · double elimination · bracket drawn entirely at random
            Loss in Upper → Lower Bracket · loss in Lower → eliminated
            BO3 everywhere except BO5: Upper Semifinals, Upper Final, Lower Semifinal, Lower Final, Grand Final
            Grand Final: single BO5, NO reset

DEFINITIONS Game = 1 match of Traversal · Series (BO) = set of games between 2 players
            BO3 = first to 2 wins · BO5 = first to 3 wins

GAME        traversalgame.com only · Public · Unranked · Random terrain
            10-min clock per player · sides assigned at random by the site
            Non-compliant setting → game not counted, replayed, warning to BOTH players

TIMING      Game ~15 min · break between games 5 min · break between series 10 min
            BO3 ≤ 55 min · BO5 ≤ 95 min · times in CET (Paris)

LATENESS    5-min allowance per game · game forfeited at H+5
            Game after a forfeit: reference +20 min (H+20, forfeit at H+25, then H+40 / H+45…)
            Series lost by forfeit = normal loss

TIES        Never a drawn game · tiebreakers in order:
            1 Σ Strength on board > 2 no. of pieces on board > 3 distance of most advanced piece
            > 4 Strength of that piece > 5 time remaining > 6 deciding game

INCIDENTS   Disconnection → clock keeps running · site bug confirmed by an admin → game fully replayed

RESULTS     Winner reports within 10 min on Discord: score + link + end screenshot of EVERY game
            Disputes ≤ 15 min after the report · Challonge = official result

PROHIBITED  Outside help · bots / AI / analysis tools · stream sniping · account sharing / multi-accounting
            Unofficial themes, designs, scripts, extensions affecting the game · result fixing

SANCTIONS   Warning → loss of a game → loss of the series → disqualification
            Cheating, unofficial tools, shared accounts, refusing the stream → immediate disqualification
```
